### 🎯 ICP 1: The "Prompt Optimizer" (AEO Audit, Credits)

**Who is:**  
Digital marketing professional, freelance SEO consultant, or small/medium business owner who heard that "ChatGPT is stealing traffic" and wants to act quickly.

**Demographics:**
- **Position:** Marketing Coordinator, SEO Specialist, Founder, Freelancer.
- **Company:** Small and medium businesses (up to ~50 employees), e-commerces, local SaaS, service businesses.
- **Digital Maturity:** Medium. They've invested in traditional SEO but never optimized for AI.

**Pain Points:**
- Noticed a drop in organic traffic from search engines but doesn't know how to adapt.
- When searching their own category in ChatGPT or Perplexity, they see competitors being cited and their brand absent.
- No budget for an AEO consulting service of R$5,000+, but needs clear technical guidance.

**Goals:**
- Receive a quick and actionable diagnosis.
- Know exactly what to fix (robots.txt, schema, content) to appear in AI responses.
- Have a score to report to boss/client and prove the site is optimized for the AI era.

**Why AIRio?**
- **Single audit with credits:** Low financial commitment (R$49 to R$249).
- **Practical results:** The report generates ready-to-download files (llms.txt, JSON-LD schema, optimized snippets).
- **CMS-specific instructions:** WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, etc. — the client doesn't need a developer to implement.

**Purchase Trigger:** "My competitor is appearing in ChatGPT and I'm not."

**JTBD:** "When I search my category in ChatGPT and see competitors cited but not my brand, I want a fast technical diagnosis with a prioritized fix list, so I can prove to my boss the site is optimized for the AI era."

**Qualifying Criteria:**
- **Must-have:** Existing site with SEO investment; aware of AI search (ChatGPT/Perplexity); can approve ~R$250 purchase independently.
- **Nice-to-have:** Uses WordPress/Webflow/Shopify; has run a GSC audit before; has a score/metric to report upward.
- **Disqualifying:** Pre-launch site (nothing to audit); expects instant ranking boost; looking for full-service agency retainer.

**Messaging:**
- ✅ Do say: "See exactly what's blocking you from appearing in ChatGPT" · "Download ready files — no developer needed" · "Score you can show your client today"
- ❌ Don't say: "Long-term content strategy" · "monthly monitoring" · "statistical confidence interval"

**Typical Journey:**  
Purchase a 10-credit package → audit 2-3 sites → implement corrections → improve score → return months later for re-audit.

### 🎯 ICP 2: The "Brand Guardian" (Visibility Monitoring, Subscription)

**Who is:**  
Medium/large company or well-funded startup whose organic traffic is vital for lead generation and revenue. Has an internal marketing team or an agency continuously handling SEO.

**Demographics:**
- **Position:** Head of Digital Marketing, CMO, Head of Growth, SEO Manager.
- **Company:** 50–500+ employees. B2B SaaS, marketplaces, fintechs, large retailers, content portals.
- **Digital Maturity:** High. Structured SEO team, uses tools like Semrush and Google Search Console.

**Pain Points:**
- Traditional search engine traffic is migrating to AI responses (zero-click searches).
- No reliable tool exists that accurately shows whether the brand is being cited in mechanisms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.
- When the brand is cited, it may be incorrect, outdated, or alongside a competitor.
- The CMO asks every month: "Are we gaining or losing space in AI search?" — and nobody has data to answer.

**Goals:**
- Have a reliable KPI for AI presence (PSOS).
- Be alerted immediately if brand visibility drops — before commercial feels revenue impact.
- Track evolution over time and correlate with internal actions (e.g., "After implementing FAQ schema, our PSOS increased 22%").
- Justify investments in content and technical SEO for AI with solid data.

**Why AIRio?**
- **Weekly monitoring with statistical rigor:** PSOS with confidence interval — not a generic rank tracker; it's a brand presence metric.
- **Drop alerts:** If the brand disappears from Perplexity responses, the CMO knows in hours, not weeks.
- **Failure diagnosis:** Not only shows it dropped, but correlates with technical problems and suggests an audit.
- **Premium localized interface:** Entire product is in Portuguese, designed for the Brazilian market.

**Purchase Trigger:** "We lost 30% of organic traffic this year and suspect AI responses are cannibalizing it."

**JTBD:** "When my CMO asks whether we're gaining or losing space in AI search and nobody has data, I want a reliable weekly PSOS metric with drop alerts, so I can quantify AI visibility and justify our content investments."

**Qualifying Criteria:**
- **Must-have:** Organic traffic is primary lead-gen channel; internal marketing team or agency managing SEO; budget authority for R$249+/month SaaS.
- **Nice-to-have:** Already uses Semrush/Ahrefs; experienced measurable traffic decline in last 12 months; CMO or board asking about AI search impact.
- **Disqualifying:** No existing SEO investment; fewer than 10 employees; looking for a one-time audit only; no recurring reporting cadence.

**Messaging:**
- ✅ Do say: "Reliable PSOS KPI for CMO reporting" · "Know before revenue feels it" · "Correlate content actions to AI visibility gains"
- ❌ Don't say: "Quick fix" · "one-time audit" · "easy setup" (they want rigor, not simplicity)

**Typical Journey:**  
Subscribe to the Pro plan (R$249/month, 3 sites) → configure strategic prompts ("best CRM for small businesses", etc.) → track PSOS weekly in monitoring dashboard → receive alerts when visibility drops → use included audit credit (when available) to fix problems.
